Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Alejandro Rincón Rodríguez, based in Spain. You can write to privacy@gridreplay.com about anything related to your data.

What data we collect and why

When you join the waitlist we store your email, the exchange where you run grid bots and, if you tick it, which tools you would like us to add. We use them only to:

  • let you know when GridReplay Pro opens and tell you about GridReplay updates (alerts and new tools);
  • understand, in aggregate, how many people are interested and which exchanges they use.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising. We never ask for API keys or access to your account on any exchange.

The bot analyzer

The history you load into the analyzer is processed inside your browser: it isn’t uploaded to GridReplay or any server, and we never see it. To run the calculation, your browser downloads the engine from this website and public prices from Binance; as with any website, Binance receives your IP address, but not your history. The simulator and the oracle work the same way: what you test or look up is calculated in your browser from public Binance data and never reaches us.

If you press “Share anonymous results”, we store only what is shown to you before sending: for each bot, the pair, direction, year, duration, number of grids and orders, and the match percentages. We don’t store amounts, exact dates, email or IP, so this data can’t identify you. We use it to extend the validation page and improve the simulator, and keep it for as long as GridReplay exists. Because we can’t tell which results are yours, we can’t delete them on request.

Affiliate links

GridReplay’s free tools are funded in part by affiliate links to exchanges, always labelled as such. If you click one, you go to the exchange’s website, which may record that you came from GridReplay with its own cookies and under its own privacy policy. GridReplay receives no personal data about you from the exchange: only aggregate figures on users and fees.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form (Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation). You can withdraw it at any time.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or, at most, 24 months from when you signed up if the product never launches.

Who else can access it

Only the technical providers we need to operate, acting as data processors: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), which hosts the website and, like any web server, logs the IP address of each visit; and Cloudflare, Inc., which stores the waitlist, the shared results and the anonymous visit counter, and forwards contact email. Both are US companies; the data transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data by writing to privacy@gridreplay.com. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or with the data protection authority where you live.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies. To know how many people visit it, we count page visits and how often the simulator and analyzer are used with our own counter, which only adds up totals per page and day: no cookies, no IP and nothing that identifies you.
