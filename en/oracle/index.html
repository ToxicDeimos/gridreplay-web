How it works

Three rules, each checked against data

  • Direction. When Binance’s top traders are heavily long, the price tends to fall over the following weeks, and vice versa; aggressive buying points the other way. The oracle combines four measures of that positioning against the pair’s own history.
  • Range. A multiple of the expected 30-day volatility (1.5, 2 or 3 times depending on the profile), with levels half a typical daily move apart. The wider it is, the less the grid earns but the far less it loses when the market runs away.
  • Very low volatility. If the pair is in the calmest 20% of its past year, you get a warning: neutral grids lost money on average at those times.
