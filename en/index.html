What it doesn’t do

Tested data, not promises

  • It doesn’t promise profits: the oracle gives a validated average edge, published with its bad months, and the final decision is yours.
  • It doesn’t trade for you or touch your money.
  • It doesn’t ask for your API keys or access to your exchange account.
  • It doesn’t hide bad results: every simulation is compared with buy and hold, and the exchange links that fund us are always labelled as affiliate links.

FAQ

Worth knowing before you sign up

How do I know the simulations match reality?

Because we test against real bots and publish every result, order by order: 45 futures grids from a real Pionex account, liquidations included. It’s all on the validation page, including the bots that deviate and the ones we left out. Before launch we’ll extend it with bots from more users.

Do I have to share my API keys or account access?

No. Simulating and alerts only need the bot’s parameters. To analyze past bots you use the history your exchange lets you download, and it’s processed in your browser. GridReplay will never be able to trade or move your money.

Does the oracle tell me which bot to create?

It shows you, like any other visitor, which direction, range and grid count today’s data favours for each pair, with the historical validation of that method. It knows nothing about your situation or your money, so it isn’t financial advice: the decision is yours. And what worked in the past doesn’t guarantee what will happen.

Which exchanges are supported?

Simulations use real Binance prices and the fees of Pionex, Binance, Bybit or KuCoin. History analysis starts with Pionex; the waitlist decides which exchange comes next.

Is it free? What does Pro include?

The simulator, the analyzer and the oracle for BTC and ETH are free. GridReplay Pro (€4.99 a month, coming soon) unlocks the oracle for every pair, alerts by Telegram or email, and the history of suggestions. Everyone who joins the list now gets the first month free.

Some exchange links are also affiliate links: if you open your account or create your bot through them, the exchange pays us part of its fees at no cost to you. They’re always labelled and don’t affect results or suggestions.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to tell you about what’s new (alerts and upcoming tools). We don’t sell or share it, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.
