Step 1

Download your Pionex history

  1. Go to pionex.com on a computer and sign in.
  2. Click your avatar (top right) → Dashboard → in the left menu, Transaction History.
  3. Pick the period you want to analyze (from your first bot until today) and confirm the export.
  4. Pionex prepares the file and emails you, usually within minutes (check spam too). Download the .zip without opening or editing it. The analysis here is instant.

Never run bots on Pionex? Then there’s nothing to analyze yet: check the oracle to see which grid to launch, or test any setup in the simulator, no history needed.

Step 2

Drop the .zip here

The first time, your browser downloads the calculation engine (about 15 MB) and Binance’s public prices for your bots’ dates. It can take a minute or two; keep the tab open.
